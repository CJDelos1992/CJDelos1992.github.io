About

I’m an applied mathematician, data scientist, and published researcher focused on risk, insurance, and public safety. I use data and mathematical models to evaluate programs, compare financial and policy choices, and understand their consequences.

My doctoral research at Arizona State University examined insurance incentives for stronger homes, the effects of public-safety technology on reported crime, and the relationship between natural hazards and crime. I approach these questions through causal inference, statistical modeling, optimization, and economic simulation.

I have published first-author research in the American Journal of Criminal Justice and contributed to projects supported by the U.S. Department of Homeland Security and the AXA Research Fund–Swiss Re Institute partnership.

I’m based in the Chicago metropolitan area and am seeking opportunities in quantitative research and applied analytics, including insurance, risk, and public policy.
